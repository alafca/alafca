# Alejandro Afonso

Mathematician turned AI Engineer with ~5 years of experience. I build LLM agents, retrieval systems, computer vision and other ML pipelines; I handle deployment, monitoring and maintenance as well. Also data science. Currently working remotely for an AI consultancy in Madrid.

Before that, two years of clinical machine learning research at Uniklinikum Erlangen — biomarker discovery and treatment-response prediction on oncology data, which produced three publications.

And before that, almost two years at a fintech startup in Austria, where I worked on fraudulent market activity detection and built a full-stack GenAI app, shortly after the release of ChatGPT.

## Interests

- Evaluation: knowing whether a system actually works, not just whether it demos well.
- The intersection of AI and healthcare.
- Fine-tuning, alignment and monitoring of LLMs.
- Not using AI when the human element is what matters.
